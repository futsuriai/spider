How to play

Build full runs of one suit from King down to Ace. Each finished run clears off the board and counts as one of the eight suits.

  • Move: drag a card, or tap it and then tap the column it goes to. Picking up a card takes the same-suit run below it.
  • Build: a card goes on a card exactly one rank higher. Any card or run can go in an empty column.
  • Deal: tap the pile at the top left to add one face-up card to every column. Fill all empty columns first.
  • Undo: steps back one move. Expert has no undo.
  • Score: starts at 500, loses 1 per move, gains 100 per finished suit.
  • Saved: your game is kept on this iPad. Close the app any time and it picks up where you left off.
